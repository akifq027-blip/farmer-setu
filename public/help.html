❓ Frequently Asked Questions by Farmers

When you book a slot on KisanSetu, you receive an exact token number and an estimated reporting time. You do not need to stand in line with your tractor for days; simply track the live "Farmers Ahead of You" counter on your phone and arrive 30 minutes before your turn.
1. Original Aadhaar Card: Required for biometric fingerprint authentication at the entrance gate.
2. Pattadar Passbook / Land Record (1B/RoR): To verify land ownership and crop sowing details.
3. Aadhaar-Linked Bank Passbook Copy: To verify your account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
4. Token Pass: Either printed or displayed on your mobile screen.
As per Fair Average Quality (FAQ) standards:
  • Paddy (Common / Grade A): Moisture must not exceed 17%. Produce with up to 17% receives full MSP.
  • Cotton: Moisture between 8% and 12% is acceptable with standard moisture refraction discounts above 8%.
  • Maize / Wheat: Moisture should be below 14%.
Please sun-dry your grain adequately before loading onto transport vehicles.
