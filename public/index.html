Simple 5 Steps

How Crop Procurement Works

1

Register Farmer

One-time simple registration with mobile number, village, and land record ID.

2

Book Crop Slot

Select your crop (Paddy, Cotton, Wheat, etc.), quantity, center, and preferred date.

3

Get Digital Token

Receive your unique token pass with estimated reporting time.

4

Visit Center

Arrive with your produce and vehicle when your queue position is called.

5

Direct Bank Credit

Weighment slip generated instantly; MSP funds credited directly via DBT.

📋 What Documents to Bring

  • ✓
    Original Aadhaar Card
    Required at the gate for biometric farmer verification.
  • ✓
    Pattadar Passbook / Land Record
    RoR / Khasra / 1B land ownership or tenancy proof.
  • ✓
    Bank Passbook Copy (Aadhaar-Linked)
    With clear Account Number & IFSC code for direct DBT credit.
  • ✓
    Printed or Mobile Digital Token Slip
    Generated on this portal for swift gate priority entry.
