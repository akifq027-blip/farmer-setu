Traditional Mandi vs KisanSetu Direct

❌ Traditional Mandi Intermediary Chain
  • • 4 to 6 Intermediaries: Village agent → Kacha arhtiya → Mandi trader → Wholesaler → Retailer.
  • • 14% - 18% Deductions: 6% commission cut + 2% mandi cess + unrecorded weighing spillages.
  • • Unfair Weighment: Manual beam scales and uncalibrated weights leading to 3-5% yield loss.
  • • Delayed Payouts: Cash IOUs and 15 to 45-day payment cycles through money lenders.
✅ KisanSetu Direct Disintermediation Network
  • • 1 Direct Link: Farmer aggregates at community FPO Hub → Direct to Mill/Retailer.
  • • 0% Brokerage Cut: 100% of fair value credited directly to the farmer.
  • • Certified Digital Scale: Calibrated electronic weighbridges with instant printed slip.
  • • 48-Hour Bank Transfer: Direct account credit via escrow settlement.
